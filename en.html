School Management Platform — Multi-Institution SaaS

From vocational training to full school management, in one system

Originally built as a management system for vocational training centers, the project evolved into a modular SaaS platform covering 5 types of institutions: kindergarten, primary school, secondary school, private university, and vocational training center (CFP). The CFP module, the most advanced today, handles 5 user profiles (Super Admin, Director, Trainer, Cashier, Trainee) and 41 business modules, with particular attention paid to regulatory compliance — MINEFOP reporting, SND30 classification, ISO 17024-certified diplomas with verification QR codes. The four other modules are still under development.
