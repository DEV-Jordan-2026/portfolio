Sobriveille — Prepaid Electricity Credit

Know exactly what you're buying before you top up your meter

A mobile app for SOCADEL prepaid meter customers. Before each purchase, it works out the energy a given amount buys, or the amount needed for a target amount of energy, from the current rate schedule, VAT and service fees included. It tracks the month's consumption, warns before a more expensive rate kicks in, and forecasts next month's rate tier from the six-month average. Payment goes through Mobile Money, and the recharge token stays available in the app.

  • Energy-saving tips matched to the home or business
